# EcoDrone — Eco-Friendly Drone Solutions

## Project Overview
**EcoDrone** is a responsive website designed to showcase eco-friendly drone technology that supports environmental projects. It highlights features, community engagement, and subscription plans for students, researchers, and environmental enthusiasts.

The project uses **HTML5** and **CSS3**, with a dark nature-inspired theme that emphasizes sustainability and eco-technology.

---

## Design Rationale / Seed
The design decisions behind EcoDrone include:

- **Dark Nature Theme:** Dark greens and teals (#0B2B26, #114B5F, #162C2A) convey eco-friendly and natural vibes. Soft text highlights (#F0F3F5, #9BC53D) maintain readability.
- **Layout:**  
  - **Header:** Logo and subtitle for clear branding.  
  - **Hero Section:** Split layout with text on the left and drone image on the right.  
  - **Sidebar Menu:** Simple vertical navigation.  
  - **Content Sections:** About, Features, and Join form presented in card-like boxes for clarity.  
  - **Footer:** Minimal footer with brand tagline.
- **Usability:** Forms with required fields, clear labels, and intuitive structure for easy user interaction.  
- **Sustainability Concept:** Emphasis on eco-friendly drone solutions, lightweight design, and non-invasive environmental monitoring.
